The installation guide ↗
01What do I need to run Lectern?

An Apple silicon Mac running macOS 26 or later. The installation guide walks you through recording permissions and setting up your preferred transcription and AI tools.

02Can I record an online lecture?

Yes. Record your microphone or capture system audio from apps such as Zoom and your browser. macOS will ask you to grant recording permission.

03Does it work for English–Hebrew shiurim?

Yes. Lectern supports English lectures and mixed English–Hebrew shiurim, with notes shaped for each. Review generated text against the recording, especially names and source references.

04Do I need Canvas or Google Docs?

No. Both are optional. You can organize your courses and keep your lecture library in Lectern without connecting either service.

05Is Lectern free?

Lectern is free and open source. The transcription and AI providers you configure may have their own charges or subscription requirements.
